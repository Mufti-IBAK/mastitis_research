Section D: Quick Field Reference Keys
CMT Scores
N (Negative): Liquid, homogeneous, no gel formation.
T (Trace): Slight, fleeting thickening when tilted; quickly vanishes.
1 (Weak Positive): Distinct thickening/gel; no central heap formed.
2 (Distinct Positive): Immediate gel thickening, moves toward center of well.
3 (Strong Positive): Heavy, viscous gel adhering to plastic, producing a central peak.
Clinical Mastitis Grades
Grade 1 (Mild): Changes in milk only (flakes, clots, watery, blood-tinged).
Grade 2 (Moderate): Milk changes + local udder inflammation (swelling, heat, redness, pain).
Grade 3 (Severe): Milk changes + local signs + systemic illness (T > 39.5 ┬░C, depressed, recumbent).
Udder Hygiene Scale (1-4)
1: Completely clean.
2: Slightly dirty (<2% surface soiled).
3: Moderately dirty (2-30% soiled with splash/caked dirt).
4: Severely dirty (>30% covered in caked dirt/manure).
