Longitudinal Udder Health Study Data Dictionary
Definitions coding and collection procedures for the single sheet long format workbook
This dictionary corresponds exactly to the 99 columns in the worksheet UDDER_HEALTH_DATA. The unit of observation is one cow ├ù one udder quarter ├ù one visit. Every examined cow therefore contributes four rows per visit, one each for LF, RF, LR and RR. The same permanent Cow_ID must be retained across all visits and lactations.
Essential entry rules
Enter four quarter rows for every examined cow-visit. Use the same Visit_ID, Cow_ID and Lactation_ID on all four rows and change only the Quarter and quarter-specific results.
Repeat farm, cow and visit information across the four quarter rows. Enter a composite cow SCC only once per cow-visit, preferably on the LF row, to prevent double-counting.
Do not overwrite Record_Key_DERIVED, DIM_DERIVED, CMT_Positive_DERIVED or SCS_DERIVED. Extend their formulas when additional rows are added.
Blank does not mean No or zero. Use Missing_Reason whenever an expected observation is unavailable.
Collect aseptic milk samples for culture before CMT and before antimicrobial treatment. CMT is ordinal and must never be converted into an invented exact SCC.
Use ISO dates (YYYY-MM-DD), permanent identifiers and the controlled dropdown values already embedded in the workbook.

Recommended quality assurance
Train observers before the pilot and conduct blinded duplicate CMT scoring. Suggested weighted-kappa target is at least 0.70.
Use standard photographs for udder hygiene, locomotion and teat-end scoring. Keep the same scoring scale throughout the study.
Review identifiers, four-quarter completeness, dates, CMT scores, sample links and treatment dates within 24 hours of every visit.
Retain raw laboratory reports and do not replace censored SCC results with arbitrary numbers.
Document any change to definitions or permitted codes in a dated protocol amendment before applying it to new records.
